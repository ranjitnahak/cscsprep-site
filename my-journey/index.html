CSCS PREP

Engineer → Ironman → Head of Sports Science.

Two decisions changed everything. One of them was the CSCS.

BEFORE

The engineer

Electrical & Electronics Engineering. Then Infosys, as a software developer. A good job on paper.

The athlete

Cricket and volleyball in college. Then marathons. Then triathlons. Then a full Ironman.

Somewhere between the code reviews and the long runs, it got obvious. The passion wasn't in tech. It was in sport.

DECISION #1

I quit Infosys.

No safety net. Just a clear idea of what I actually wanted to do.

THE CLIMB

  3. Lead Trainer
  4. →

  6. →
  7. Head of the S&C Format

Good growth. But I wanted elite sport, and there was a ceiling.

DECISION #2

The CSCS.

So I went after the CSCS.

Studied it. Cleared it.
